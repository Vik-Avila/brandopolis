# Social posts and ad pieces

Adapted from Marketing Skills `social`, `ad-creative` and `ads` (see [PROVENANCE](PROVENANCE.md)). Platform limits change:
treat any limit as «to verify against the provider's current documentation» before shipping it in product copy or tests.

## Social posts (Marketing › Redes sociales)

- Each post has objective, network, format, text, materials from the published kit, review and a **manual**
  publication record. Brandopolis does not publish to networks in Phase 4.
- First line carries the hook; the rest earns the action. One idea per post.
- Repurpose from approved sources (offer, positioning, published guide), never from another brand.
- Calendar entries are drafts until a person approves them; editing an approved snapshot needs a new version.
- Accessibility: alternative text for images, captions for video, no information only in colour or only in emoji.

## Ad pieces (Marketing › Campañas digitales)

- Start from the campaign objective and the **approved** offer version and landing page; the piece must keep the
  promise the page fulfils (message match).
- Variations vary one idea at a time (benefit, proof, objection, audience moment) so a result means something.
- Google Search responsive ads: write headlines that stand alone in any order; no headline depends on another.
  Keep the brand name and the main benefit available among the pinned options only when needed.
- Meta: the first sentence of the primary text and the visual carry the message; text in images stays short.
- Regulated categories (health, finance, housing, employment, politics, alcohol) and claims about results go to
  a human before anything else.
- Never write a piece that implies a price, discount, guarantee or date that is not in the approved offer.

## Review of a piece

Answer per check: seen and right, seen and wrong, not verifiable, not applicable.

1. Does it match the approved offer version and the landing page?
2. Is every claim backed by approved evidence?
3. Does it follow the published kit (colours, type, logo use) where the format uses it?
4. Is the call to action one, clear and possible on the destination?
5. Is it free of the machine-sounding patterns in [copy](copy.md)?

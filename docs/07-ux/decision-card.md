# Strategic Decision Card

Componente central del Strategic Workspace: Question, Why this matters, What we know (Evidence), What we assume (Hypothesis vinculada como Assumption in Use), Options, Recommendation, Why/Rationale, Trade-offs, Support (Strong/Moderate/Limited/Unvalidated), Approve/Modify/Reject. Progressive disclosure sin esconder procedencia ni Needs Review. Approval abre revisión de elección/rationale y emite commit humano; Modify conserva diferencia de Recommendation; Reject no crea Decision. Mostrar conflictos de versión y estado AI unavailable. Home y jornadas en [product-journeys](product-journeys.md); estados en [states](states-and-empty-states.md).
